Facts (source): latent heat of fusion of water. Water releases about 334 J/g as it freezes (latent heat of fusion
of ice, ~333.55 J/g at 0 °C), so a tub of water freezing in a closed cellar holds the air near 0 °C until the
water has frozen through. Farmers used tubs of water in root cellars for exactly this (frost protection for stored
potatoes and apples; also the reason growers spray orchards with water before a frost).
