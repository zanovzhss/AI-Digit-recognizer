# MandelShort: shot plan (whole video)

**"The Man Who Won the Lottery 14 Times"**: 53.94 s (1618 frames at 30 fps), voice `mandel-short/voice/voice.wav`.
Times are the word onsets in `mandel-short/voice/words.json`. Picture events land 0-2 frames before their word.

Rules from `research/ricks/STYLE_REFERENCE.md` (section 8 targets apply):
- **Camera:** each shot glides over its first 30-40 %, then holds and creeps in at 0.08-0.12 %/frame, with no float.
  The camera moves in ~40-45 % of frames, holds average ~11 frames, and some holds run 30-45 frames.
- **Changes:** a place or time change is an 8-16-frame crossfade, or a push-in to a prop insert (the prop fills
  50-80 % of the frame) that crossfades into the next scene and then pulls back. Hard cuts are rare; whips ≤ 2.
- **Light:** day / golden / night / interior. Night and interior scenes are lit by visible warm practicals with light
  pools. The grade saturation is ~1.0-1.05, and day shadows are warm grey (#504b4d).
- **Sets:** ≥ 15 props in interiors, ≥ 8 outdoors, 1-3 story signs per place, and no flat colour over 35 % of the frame.

Legend for the "Rules" column: **R3** = crossfade or prop-insert change · **R7** = signs and the callback counter ·
**R8** = the explanation set · **R9** = staging (OTS = over-the-shoulder with a blurred foreground head, LOW = low
angle, TOP = top-down, WIDE-CU = wide-lens close-up, CROWD = 60+ extras).
